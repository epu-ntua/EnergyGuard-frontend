About this export

This file is a readable copy of personal_data.json, which holds the same data in a machine-readable format. Your profile picture, if you uploaded one, is in the profile_picture folder.

Not included:

  • The contents of your dataset files and digital twin results. They are listed above and can be downloaded from the platform.
  • Personal data of other people, such as the members of your team or the collaborators on your projects.
  • Your sign-in account (Keycloak), which holds the same name and email address shown above.

To have your data deleted, use the Privacy tab of your EnergyGuard profile.
