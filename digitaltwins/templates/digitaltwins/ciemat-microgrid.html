CIEMAT Microgrid

Organization: CIEMAT Location: Soria, Spain Domain: Microgrids & Renewable Energy

The CIEMAT TEF node is located at the Centre for Renewable Energy Development (CEDER-CIEMAT) in Soria, Spain, and provides a real-world testing and experimentation environment for the development and validation of AI-enabled services for renewable energy systems and smart microgrids. The node comprises a hybrid microgrid integrating photovoltaic, wind and hydropower generation, energy storage systems, electric vehicles with V2G capability, flexible loads and an advanced monitoring and control infrastructure. The microgrid can operate in both grid-connected and dedicated experimental configurations, enabling the evaluation of innovative energy management strategies under realistic operating conditions.

Complementing the physical infrastructure, the node provides a digital experimentation environment where AI-based forecasting, optimisation, digital twin and energy management services are developed and validated. Through its integration with the EnergyGuard platform, the node offers secure and interoperable access to data, forecasting services and experimentation capabilities.

  • Renewable Generation Forecasting Service
    AI-based day-ahead forecasting of photovoltaic and wind generation, combining Numerical Weather Prediction data with historical and real-time measurements from the microgrid.
  • Renewable Energy Scenario Analysis Service
    Configuration and evaluation of renewable energy integration scenarios and flexibility resources, such as batteries and electric vehicles, under different operating conditions.
  • Digital Twin Experimentation Service
    Simulation and analysis of renewable energy systems, with customisable AI model parameters, before implementing changes in the physical microgrid.
